Computer help in Lowell, MA for PC and Mac

Route 3 Tech offers calm, practical PC and Mac tune-ups for Lowell, Chelmsford, Burlington, and Nashua. If your computer is slow, unstable, or overdue for a reliable backup, this visit gets it back on track.

The goal is simple: fix what is slowing you down, improve stability, and make sure your data is protected. You get clear explanations and a tidy setup that stays fast after I leave.

Flat-rate tune-up with up to 2 hours in person

The PC or Mac tune-up plus backup is a flat rate that includes up to 2 hours in person. That time is used to clean up performance issues, update software, and set up a backup you can trust.

  • Speed and stability checks for Windows or macOS
  • Updates, cleanup, and basic safety review
  • Backup setup, testing, and simple guidance
  • Notes on what changed and what to watch next

If you want, I will also show a few easy habits that keep things fast, like safe storage cleanup and simple update timing.

Common computer issues I fix

  • Slow startups, freezes, or noisy fans
  • Storage nearly full or running out of space
  • Updates that keep failing or never finish
  • Unwanted pop-ups, junk apps, or confusing toolbars
  • File organization that makes backups unreliable

Good fit for home and small office setups

This tune-up is ideal for home offices, family laptops, and small business workstations that need steady performance. I can optimize a single computer or look at how it connects to printers, external drives, and shared folders. If hardware limits the results, I will explain what to replace and what can wait so you can plan without pressure.

What to expect from intake to follow-up

  1. Quick intake

    Describe what is happening, your town, and whether you prefer remote or in-person help.

  2. On-site tune-up

    I address the main issues, confirm backup health, and review any hardware limits within the 2-hour visit.

  3. Simple wrap-up

    You get a short summary of improvements, plus next steps if replacements are worth considering.

  4. Follow-up support

    Seven-day follow-up by text and one remote check-in are included.
